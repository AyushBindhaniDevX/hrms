SECTION 1: FOR EMPLOYEES

👤 What Can Employees Do?

As an employee, Oasis is your personal portal for daily work life. Everything you need is accessible from your mobile phone or web browser in just a few taps.

📍
1-Tap Smart Clock In / Out
Menu: Attendance
Daily

When you reach the office, open the app. The app verifies that you are inside the office building using GPS.

  • Live Distance: Shows how close you are (e.g. "15 meters away — Inside Office").
  • Quick Selfie: Front camera takes a quick snap to confirm it's you.
  • Break Timer: Pause for Tea or Lunch with 1 tap.
  • Monthly History: See your full month calendar of present, late, or off days.
🏖️
Apply for Leaves in 30 Seconds
Menu: Leave
Self-Service

No more sending emails or filling paper forms to take time off.

  • Live Balances: View your remaining Annual, Sick, and Casual leave days.
  • Half-Day Option: Select First-Half or Second-Half if needed.
  • Instant Notifications: Get notified as soon as HR approves your leave.
  • Holiday List: Check all upcoming festival and company holidays.
💵
View Salary & Download Payslips
Menu: Salary / Payslips
Monthly

Access your monthly salary breakdown securely whenever you need it.

  • Clear Earnings: See Basic pay, HRA, and allowances clearly.
  • Deductions: Check PF, Taxes (TDS), and take-home pay.
  • 1-Click Download: Download or share official digital payslips for loans or records.
🤖
Ask "Ovi" (24/7 AI HR Assistant)
Menu: Call Ovi
AI Powered

Got a question about company policy, leaves, or health insurance? Talk or chat with Ovi!

  • Instant Answers: "How many sick leaves do I have left?"
  • Policy Guide: "What is our travel reimbursement policy?"
  • Voice Call: Tap to call Ovi for a friendly voice conversation.

More Employee Self-Service Tools

🧾 Expense Claims

Took a client to lunch or bought office supplies? Snap a photo of the bill, enter the amount, and get reimbursed directly.

🎓 Training & LMS

Watch skill-building video lessons, complete mandatory security courses, and earn downloadable certificates.

🤝 Kudos & Recognition

Appreciate your teammates by sending fun Kudos badges ("Rockstar", "Team Player") on the social feed.

SECTION 2: FOR HR MANAGERS

💼 What Can HR Managers Do?

Oasis eliminates repetitive paperwork so HR teams can focus on people, hiring, and culture. Here are the core operational workflows you can run every day:

Daily & Monthly HR Workflows

1

Live Attendance Radar (Who is in, late, or on leave)

At /(hr)/attendance, view real-time statistics of the entire company. See who punched in on time, who is running late with shift grace periods, and who is away on approved leave.

2

1-Click Leave Approvals

At /(hr)/leave, review all pending employee leave requests in one queue. Check the employee's reason and remaining balance, then click Approve or Reject with a single tap.

3

3-Step Monthly Payroll & Salary Processing

At /(hr)/payroll, open the current month's payroll. The system automatically calculates working days, cuts for unpaid leaves (LOP), and generates digital payslips for all staff members automatically.

4

Hiring & Applicant Tracking (ATS)

At /(hr)/recruitment, create job postings that automatically appear on your public Careers page (/careers), LinkedIn, and job boards. Move candidates across interview rounds, score them, send offer letters, and turn them into employees with 1 click.

HR Operational Hub Overview

💻
Company Asset & Laptop Registry

Keep track of all company laptops, monitors, phones, and hardware. Know who has which device, serial numbers, purchase values, and warranty dates.

🎯
Goals & 360 Performance Reviews

Set company and department goals (OKRs) with progress sliders. Run annual or quarterly appraisal cycles where employees submit self-reviews and managers provide ratings.

⏰
Shifts & Roster Planning

Create Morning, Evening, or Night shifts with custom start/end times and grace allowances (e.g. 15 mins). Assign shifts to staff members individually or by department.

🎫
Helpdesk & Ticket Management

Receive and resolve internal staff requests for HR questions, IT support, payroll queries, or office facilities with priority flags (Urgent, High, Normal).

SECTION 3: ADMIN & QUICK REFERENCE

👑 For Business Owners & Admins

The Admin console gives leadership complete visibility, company branding controls, office location setup, and security oversight.

📊 Executive Dashboard

Instant birds-eye view of total headcount, active departments, daily attendance percentages, and monthly payroll expenses.

🏢 Office & Geofence Setup

Add your company office branches, set their map location (GPS coordinates), and set how far staff can be to punch in (e.g. 150m radius).

🎨 Company Branding

Upload your company logo, customize the company name, set default working hours, official currency, and timezones.

🛡️

Enterprise Security & Audit Trail

Every important action in the system (changing salaries, promoting staff, changing office coordinates, or deleting entries) is permanently recorded in the Audit Logs for complete transparency.
