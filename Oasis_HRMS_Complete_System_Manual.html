OASIS HRMS MANUAL SECTION 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE

1. Executive Summary & Tech Architecture

Oasis HRMS is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant Human Capital Management & Workforce Automation platform engineered with a single unified codebase supporting Web, Android, and iOS. Built on React Native + Expo SDK and powered by Supabase PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS) and real-time synchronization.

Frontend Engine
Expo Router (file-based), React 19, TypeScript, React Native Web, Victory Native charts, Skia graphics.
Cloud & Database
Supabase PostgreSQL 15, Row-Level Security (RLS), Realtime WebSocket feeds, Edge Storage buckets.
AI & Smart Services
Google Gemini AI ("Ovi Voice Assistant"), Haversine GPS Geofencing, Facial biometrics, Resend Email API.

OASIS HRMS MANUAL SECTION 2: CORE WORKFORCE & ATTENDANCE MODULES

2. In-Depth Functional Modules (Where & How)

2.1 Smart GPS Geofence & Biometric Attendance

📍 Location: /(employee)/attendance (Employee) & /(hr)/attendance (HR/Admin)
Oasis prevents buddy-punching using a multi-factor attendance verification engine combining device GPS coordinates with server-side Haversine geodesic distance validation against workplace geofence perimeters, coupled with front-camera facial snapshot verification.
Employee Capabilities
  • 1-Tap Smart Punch: Instant Clock In and Clock Out with live GPS distance meter (e.g. "12m from Office 1 - Inside Geofence").
  • Face Recognition Snap: Seamless front camera capture storing biometric verification logs.
  • Break Tracking: Record tea, lunch, and custom breaks with accurate pause timers.
  • Attendance History: Monthly calendar view indicating Present, Late, Half-day, Overtime, and Leave.
  • Regularization Requests: Raise attendance adjustment requests with explanatory notes for forgotten punches.
HR & Admin Management
  • Organization Live Radar: Monitor daily presence %, who is on-time, late, on leave, or absent in real-time.
  • Verification Logs: Review GPS latitude/longitude coordinates and camera snapshot proof for every punch.
  • Workplace Office Geofencing: Configure offices at /(hr)/locations with custom GPS coordinates and radius (e.g., 50m to 500m).
  • Shift Linkage: Automatic shift grace period calculations (e.g., 15 mins allowance) before marking status as "Late".

2.2 Leave Management & Quota Automation

🏖️ Location: /(employee)/leave & /(hr)/leave
Automated entitlement management with dynamic quota tracking, multi-tier approvals, and automatic balance reconciliation.
Leave Categories Supported
  • Annual / Privilege Leave (AL): 18 days default paid vacation.
  • Sick / Medical Leave (SL): 12 days medical coverage.
  • Casual Leave (CL): 7 days short personal leave.
  • Maternity / Paternity: 90 days maternity / 7 days paternity.
  • Compensatory Off (Comp-Off): 5 days for overtime/weekend shifts.
Workflows & Logic
  • Dynamic Balances: Real-time deduction upon approval; instant refund upon cancellation of pending requests.
  • Half-Day Mode: Allows First-Half or Second-Half 0.5-day leave applications.
  • HR Approval Queue: Approve or Reject with mandatory manager remarks and automated email alert.
  • Holiday Calendar: Integrates statutory public and regional holidays directly into the leave planner.

2.3 Payroll, Tax Deductions & Payslips

💰 Location: /(hr)/payroll & /(employee)/payslips
End-to-end compensation engine calculating gross pay, loss of pay (LOP) deductions, statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS), and digital payslip delivery.
Payroll Component Calculation Type Description
Basic Salary Fixed Base (e.g. 50%) Core taxable monthly salary defined per employee in staff master
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Allowance (e.g. 40-50%) Tax-exempt allowance under statutory guidelines
Special / Travel Allowances Variable / Fixed Medical, conveyance, night-shift allowances (calculated from shift logs)
Loss of Pay (LOP) Automated Deduction (Basic Salary / Total Month Days) * Unapproved Absent Days
Provident Fund (PF) & TDS Statutory Deductions 12% PF contribution + withholding income tax calculations
Net Salary Disbursed Gross Pay - Total Deductions Final take-home pay generated with a unique digital Payslip ID
OASIS HRMS MANUAL SECTION 3: ATS, PERFORMANCE, LMS & OPERATIONS

3. Talent, Learning & Operational Modules

3.1 Enterprise ATS & Recruitment Pipeline

🎯 Location: /(hr)/recruitment & Public Careers Portal /careers
Full-cycle recruitment suite from job requisition creation to multi-channel publishing, Kanban applicant tracking, interview scorecards, and 1-click employee onboarding.
1

Job Requisition & Publishing

Create detailed job openings with salary ranges, experience, skills, perks, and target join date. Publish to Oasis Public Careers portal (/careers), LinkedIn, Indeed, and Naukri.

2

Candidate Sourcing & Screening

External applicants apply via the public careers page. Resumes, portfolio links, and KYC are ingested into the ATS Kanban board across custom stages.

3

Interview Rounds & Structured Scoring

Schedule multi-round interviews (Technical, System Design, HR). Interviewers submit structured ratings (1-5 stars) and qualitative feedback.

4

Digital Offer Letter & Auto-Hire

Issue formal offer letters with compensation breakdown. Once accepted, HR clicks "Convert to Employee" to automatically generate their user account and staff profile.

4.2 Enterprise Security, Governance & Audit Trails

Every single table in PostgreSQL is protected by strict RLS policies ensuring complete multi-tenant and departmental isolation.

Comprehensive Audit Logs
/(admin)/audit-logs

Tracks all privileged actions (role changes, salary updates, geofence modifications) with actor ID, IP address, and JSON diffs.
